# v0.8 step 1: root causes and fixes

## A. Red box around every video
**Cause:** the Editor's safe-zone guide. It's a red/pink striped overlay over the top, bottom and right-hand
button areas, and it was **switched on by default** (TikTok), so every clip looked boxed in red. It was only ever
drawn in the page; nothing red was burned into exports. A new test renders every layout × caption template and
proves there are no red or guide-line pixels in any exported file.

**Fix:** guides are off by default. When you turn one on, it's a thin neutral dashed outline labelled "guide only",
drawn by the page on top of the preview, never by the renderer.

## B. Captions moving all over the place
1. **Main cause:** each caption chunk recalculated its position from the layout on screen at that moment. The AI
   reframe switches layout mid-clip (split → crop → speaker → blur), so captions jumped between the seam
   (~y 710) and the lower third (~y 1230).
2. A two-line chunk near the edge of its area was pushed down about 40 px, so captions bounced between one-line
   and two-line chunks.
3. The pop animation scaled the active word *inside* the line, so its neighbours slid sideways during each pop.
4. The Editor preview drew captions with HTML while exports used libass. They never matched exactly.

**Fix:**
- There's one caption position per clip, taken from the layout that's on screen longest (the seam for split
  clips).
- The centre is fixed once for the whole clip.
- The pop is drawn as its own layer centred on the word, scaled around the word's own centre. The line itself
  never moves.
- The preview now uses **the export renderer**: a low-res render of the current version, plus libass-drawn stills
  of the current frame while it rebuilds. Tests check:
  - the caption centre stays within ±1% over 30 s with 6 layout switches and 6 zooms;
  - preview stills match export frames (mean difference under 3/255; measured 0.02).

## C. Wrong moments (sad, slow or random gameplay)
Measured on test segments (score 0–1, before → after):

| Segment | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Funny (laughing + KEKW chat) | 0.84 | 0.85 |
| Explosion + laughter | 0.82 | 0.84 |
| **Sad talk, chat sending "F / o7 / Sadge"** | **0.55** (picked in the top 3) | **0.00** |
| **Calm driving** | **0.19** | **0.00** |
| Calm talk, menu music | 0.00 | 0.00 |

Causes:
1. **The LLM rating rewarded emotion.** The score was `max(funny, hype, stop_scroll)`. "Stop scroll" and
   "shocking, intense" both rate sad or serious talk highly, and nothing counted against it.
2. **Sad chat counted as hype.** "F", "rip" and "Sadge" were in the *fail* reaction list, so condolences made chat
   look excited.
3. **Wrong YAMNet class mapping:**
   - "Crowd" (constant GTA street ambience) counted as cheering;
   - "Car passing by", revving and "Race car" counted as action, so driving around scored;
   - "Burst, pop", "Bang" and "Thump, thud" fired on music drums and footsteps;
   - crying and sighing weren't listened for at all.
4. **Fallback typing:** a moment with no signal was labelled Action/Explosion, or Wholesome if anyone was talking.

**Fixes in this build:**
- **LLM:** the rating prompt now rewards entertainment (funny, chaos, shock) and returns a **serious** score that
  counts against the moment. Serious ≥ 6 with low fun halves the score.
- **Chat:** condolence chat is its own "sad" signal that damps the chat score.
- **YAMNet:** the class mapping is corrected, with new *driving* and *sad* groups.
- **Penalties:** new penalties for calm driving and sad/serious moments.

The full rebuild (a learned model from real viral clips, event tags, reaction × event interactions) is step 2.
